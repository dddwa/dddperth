One of the best things about DDD Perth is that it provides a forum for people who have never presented at a conference before to have a chance to speak. The anonymous voting process used by DDD Perth ensures that everyone that submits a presentation abstract has the same chance of being selected to present.

So, if you’re on the fence about presenting at DDD Perth, here are a few reasons why it’s a good idea:

## 1\. Gain presenting experience

Invariably as you move through your career you will be called upon to present your ideas to potential customers, fellow team mates or as part of a marketing exercise. Presenting at DDD Perth gives you an opportunity to gain some experience presenting something to a large group in a safe and friendly atmosphere.

## 2\. Further your understanding

After you’ve written your abstract and it’s been selected for the conference you’re going to spend time preparing your presentation. This means spending time to dive really deep into the topic you’re presenting to make sure that you really understand it and can teach other people about it. This works as a great motivator for something you’ve always been passionate about, but never committed the time to “dive deep” into.

## 3\. Expand your network

When you present on a topic at DDD Perth you’re demonstrating your keen interest in a particular topic. Attendees that are also interested in the same topic may ask questions during the Q&A period, or catch you in between talks or at the after party to have a more in-depth discussion.

This is a great chance for you to meet other people in the local community who are interested in the same things as you — who knows, you might make a new friend or find your next job!

## 4\. New opportunities

DDD Perth attracts a very diverse group of people! You never know; your future co-founder might be watching. Conversely being able to list the presentations you’ve given at conferences might be the edge you need when applying for that promotion you’ve been wanting!

## 5\. Marketing

Every presentation starts with a short bio about the presenter, use this to get the word out about the interesting problems you’re solving as part of your job. This gets exposure for your organisation helping fill those vacant positions that you’ve been trying to fill. Even better if you can claim a finder’s fee!

## 6\. Build a Personal Brand

By presenting on a particular subject you can start to build a personal brand in that subject. A great local example of that can be found in one of our organisers, Jake Ginnivan, who is very effective at building a personal brand by speaking at conferences and user groups. If you are interested in building a personal brand then we highly encourage you to talk to the likes of Jake at the conference.

The reasons people present at DDD Perth are as many and diverse as the attendees. If you’re a first-time presenter the DDD Perth team is willing to help you prepare your abstract and provide constructive feedback on your presentation before the day, so really — you’ve got nothing to lose! Head on over to the DDD Perth website to find out more information, and while you’re there grab a ticket: [https://dddperth.com/](https://dddperth.com/).
