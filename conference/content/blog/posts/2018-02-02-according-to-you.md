Following DDD Perth 2017, a bunch of attendees gave the internet their take on our conference. Apart from becoming one of the top five trending topics in Australia for conference day…

> TagsTrends on Twitter
>
> [View on Twitter](https://twitter.com/_tagstrends_/status/909633257880375301)

…there were also some *blushingly* complimentary posts about the conference.

If perchance you missed one, we’ve rounded them up for you. Take a look!

---

### Kris Howard

> Thank you again to the DDD Perth organisers for inviting me to participate in this wonderful event!

[Kris](https://medium.com/u/4fdbe354f19b), we’re the ones who are thanking you for delivering such an inspiring locknote. Catch the video of Kris’ talk [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zMReShyytQA&t=2s).

[DDD Perth 2017](https://www.web-goddess.org/archive/17875)

### Dash Digital

> They put on a great event that was well-priced at only $50 a ticket, and included top-notch speakers, lunch and networking opportunities at the afterparty.

What we love about this round up from [Dash](https://twitter.com/dashperth) is the coverage of three of our speakers, [Kris Howard](https://medium.com/u/4fdbe354f19b), [Nathan Jones](https://medium.com/u/58eac449f197) and [Will Webster](https://twitter.com/willwebst).

[Dash developers do #DDDPerth](https://www.dashdigital.com.au/blog/item/dash-developers-do-dddperth.html)

### Nathan Jones

> It was a credit to the organisers and a shout-out to the sponsors for helping make it happen.

We love how [Nathan](https://medium.com/u/58eac449f197) invites future conversations and connections. As he says in the blog post, his [DM’s are open](https://twitter.com/the_nathanjones)! You can catch Nathan’s talk [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fWN2zysDySQ).

[DDD Perth Wrap-Up](https://medium.com/@thenathanjones/ddd-perth-wrap-up-c885086c0642)

### Gaia Resources

> Having the opportunity to hear some of Perth’s best developers talk about their experiences and recommendations is an excellent opportunity to get away from our day-to-day work and find some fresh perspective.

Fun fact: Six developers from [Gaia](https://twitter.com/gaiaresources) turned out to represent at DDD Perth. Talk about commitment to learning!

[Serverless Architecture at the Small Scale - Gaia Resources](https://www.gaiaresources.com.au/ddd-perth-2017/)

### LiveHire

> Thank you, DDD Perth for creating such a worthwhile and exciting event.

[LiveHire](https://twitter.com/Livehireme) is a continued proud sponsor of DDD Perth and we couldn’t do what we do without them. Thanks for the round up!

[LiveHire @ Developer Developer Developer! Perth Event 👏 #DDDPerth](https://blog.livehire.com/livehire-developer-developer-developer-perth-event-dddperth/)

### Amy Kapernick

> Despite the small price tag and the fact that I had to get up early on a Saturday, not only did it not disappoint but I was blown away by the number of supporters, the calibre of the speakers and the overall experience.

[Amy’s](https://twitter.com/Amys_Kapers) glowing review blew us out of the water. Thanks for the kind words, Amy - you keep us motivated for 2018!

[Amy Goes to Perth](https://amygoestoperth.com.au/76186f42c651)

### Donna Edwards

> I was also really impressed with the diversity at the conference and how the committee had really made a huge effort to attract more women speakers

[Donna’s](https://twitter.com/Don_IsGood) talk was extremely informative; you can check it out [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LK-3YtMIel8). Donna herself organises large scale events and we really value her feedback on DDD!

[DDD Perth 2017](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ddd-perth-2017-donna-edwards)
