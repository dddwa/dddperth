The last 6 months at [DDD Perth](https://medium.com/u/874d3f91c5ba) has seen us hit some amazing milestones:

- Becoming the largest software conference in Perth with over 300 registered attendees
- [Trending on Twitter](https://twitter.com/DDDPerth/status/910004113768800256) for the conference day
- Blind submissions and voting for the 2017 conference (did you see our [kick-ass agenda](http://dddperth.com/agenda/2017) that came from this??)
- Incorporation of DDD WA Inc with a capable and enthusiastic board of directors, ready for 2018!

DDD Perth started in 2015, and two dedicated people, [Robert Daniel Moore](https://medium.com/u/1fc793d984a5) and [Matt Davies](https://twitter.com/mdaviesnet) have been driving the conference forward each and every year. In 2017, we spread the load amongst a wider group, and in 2018 we’ve formalised the conference with the incorporation of DDD WA Inc. It’s the DDD Perth committee you know and love, with a new name (for the association only) and a big ‘ole cup of motivation for the future.

So, let’s meet the team involved in DDD Perth for 2018.

### Lee Campbell

[Lee Campbell (@LeeRyanCampbell) \| Twitter](https://twitter.com/LeeRyanCampbell)

Lee describes himself as an angry, messy, intolerant dev who has a compelling need to contribute to the community to make up for his sins. He’s a freelance developer, author and presenter with extensive experience in concurrent programming, composite applications, web and integration technologies.\
