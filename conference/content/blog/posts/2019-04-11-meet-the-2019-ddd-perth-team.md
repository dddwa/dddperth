### DDD Perth 2018 was record breaking and we’re back again this year for more!

Our 2018 conference had the largest attendance to date, more speaker submissions than we’ve ever received and our biggest number of tracks.

2019 is going to be an even more exciting and jam-packed year for us and you! The team is taking in feedback from last year and opening up even more speaking opportunities and bringing in a whole new range of topics and events leading into the 2019 conference.

Let’s meet the team that’s going to make this happen:

## **Rebecca Waters**

![Rebecca Waters](/images/blog/2019-04-11-meet-the-2019-ddd-perth-team/02.jpeg)

*[Rebecca Waters](https://au.linkedin.com/in/rebeccacwaters)*

Rebecca is a software engineer and project manager who feeds off the enthusiasm of others and contributes to the Perth software industry. She is a mentor in and outside of her company to junior developers and other professionals and chair of the Australian Computer Society — Diversity & Inclusion chapter. Rebecca’s vision for DDD WA is to be the place you want to be at, and DDD Perth the conference you can’t afford to miss.
