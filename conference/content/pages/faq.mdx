<OutsideConferenceDay>
    <details>
        <summary>What if I can't afford to attend?</summary>

        If you can't afford the ticket price, we have limited Sponsored (Financial Assistance) tickets available. We also have
        an option for people to donate further tickets. The only requirement for eligibility is that you can't afford
        the ticket.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Is this just for software developers?</summary>

        No! While our name implies we are just about devs, our events are aimed at all professionals in the software
        industry - developers, testers, designers, analysts, managers, etc.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>How is the agenda chosen?</summary>

        The event is a community-driven event with core values of inclusion and democratic engagement. Proposed sessions
        are anonymized and voted for by the public, but some curation is required to produce an agenda that meets our
        inclusion goals.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will refreshments be provided?</summary>

        Yes, attendees will receive morning tea, lunch and afternoon tea. This year, we are providing all attendees a
        token to redeem for one free coffee, and you will be able to purchase coffee and soft drinks throughout the day.
        Note: purchases are via card only, as Optus Stadium is a cashless venue.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will there be wifi?</summary>

        Yes, wifi will be available at the venue.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will childcare be available?</summary>

        Yes! Childcare is available for the duration of the main conference for those that have pre-booked tickets.
        Please talk to our registration staff on the day who will escort your child to childcare. Note: Childcare
        allocations are exhausted for 2026.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>How do I pay for my tickets?</summary>

        Payments can be made with credit card using Tito via our tickets page when registrations are open.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Can I cancel/give my ticket to someone else?</summary>

        You are welcome to send someone else in your place. Please do this through Tito. As a NFP we rely on ticket
        sales to fund the conference and so we do not offer refunds.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>What is the hashtag for the event?</summary>

        The Twitter hashtag is [#DDDPerth](https://x.com/hashtag/DDDPerth).
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will I be photographed or filmed?</summary>

        Media personnel authorized by the event will be in attendance. These media personnel will respect the photo
        policy as defined in the Code of Conduct.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>I want to be involved. Can I help?</summary>

        We are always looking for volunteers and sometimes looking for organizers! Email us at [info@dddperth.com](mailto:info@dddperth.com) and we
        can work with you to figure out the best way to assist. Note: We have filled our volunteer positions for 2026.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>How can I contact the organizers?</summary>

        We can be contacted via email at [info@dddperth.com](mailto:info@dddperth.com). Also, see our various social media accounts at the footer of
        this page.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>How can I sponsor the event?</summary>

        The event offers a unique marketing and recruiting opportunity. We have various levels of sponsorship available.
        If you would like a copy of the sponsorship prospectus, see our [sponsorship page](/sponsorship).
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Who are the organizers?</summary>

        The event is organized by DDD Perth, a non-profit organization set up to create inclusive events for the WA
        software community.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Can I wear a face mask?</summary>

        Absolutely, we support any attendees who choose to wear a face mask on the day of the conference.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will you have sanitizer available at the conference?</summary>

        Yes! Optus Stadium offers sanitizing processes in place for all attendees on the day, and this includes
        providing hand sanitizer. We encourage everyone to bring their own personal supply too.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>I don’t feel well, can I still come to the conference?</summary>

        If you’re experiencing any cold or flu-like symptoms, please stay home.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Will talks be available online after the conference?</summary>

        Yes! We’ll be releasing recordings of the talks so you can catch up on anything you may have missed on the day.
    </details>

</OutsideConferenceDay>

<ConferenceDay>
    <details>
        <summary>Is there a DDD Perth app?</summary>

        There’s an app from previous years and it should still work, but we haven’t made any updates to it for 2026. If you already have it installed you’re welcome to keep using it — if something doesn’t look right, check this website, since that’s where we’ll be posting updates on the day.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Where will updates or changes on the day be communicated?</summary>

        Updates and changes on the day will be shared on this website and announced at the info / help desk. If you have the app from previous years installed, announcements still come through there too. Keep an eye on the agenda for the latest session and room details.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Who do I contact if I need help?</summary>

        DDD Perth EVENT CREW are wearing bright branded T-shirts – you can reach out to any of them on the day for assistance OR you can go to the info / help desk located at Lobby 135.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>If I need support with accessibility where do I go?</summary>

        Please go to the info / help desk located at Lobby 135.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>If I provided Dietary requirements where do I get my meal?</summary>

        Go to Food Zone 4 in the Premiership Terrace past the Sports Lounge, tell them you have a dietary requirement and provide your name. This applies to those that have specified a dietary requirement in the ticket purchasing process.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Where do I go if I lose something?</summary>

        Please report it at the info / help desk located at Lobby 135.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>How do I report an issue or complaint?</summary>

        During the day if you have an issue that can be remedied please speak to the EVENT CREW in bright branded T-shirts or go to the info/help desk. If it is something to report to the committee for future remediation please email [info@dddperth.com](mailto:info@dddperth.com) and we will get back to you after the event.
    </details>

    <details>

    <details>
        <summary>If I can’t attend at the last minute due to illness or an emergency what can I do?</summary>

        You can transfer your ticket to another person using the links on the email you received with your ticket – or if you are not able to figure this out you can forward them the email with your ticket and cc [info@dddperth.com](mailto:info@dddperth.com).
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Where and what is the Quiet room?</summary>

        The quiet room is a space where you can sit on a couch and take a break from ‘peopleing’. Follow the signs to find it, or ask any of our volunteer team.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>Is there Childcare available?</summary>

        Childcare has sold out and is no longer available.
    </details>

    <details>
        <summary>What time does DDD Perth finish?</summary>

        The end plenary session finishes at approximately 5pm and we will be directed to exit Optus stadium directly after this. 
    </details>
