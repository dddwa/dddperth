## Josh Sewell - Chair

<Image src="/images/team/josh-sewell.jpeg" alt="Josh Sewell" size="portrait" />
<p>
    Josh serves as the Chair and Treasurer for DDD Perth, providing leadership and managing the financial aspects of the

    the co-sell motion and solving customers' biggest problems across the region.
</p>
