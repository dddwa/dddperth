## Josh Sewell - Chair

<Image src="/images/team/josh-sewell.jpeg" alt="Josh Sewell" size="portrait" />

Josh serves as the Chair and Treasurer for DDD Perth, providing leadership and managing the financial aspects of the conference. Currently working as the Asia Pacific and Japan Partner Manager for Salesforce at AWS, Josh is growing the co-sell motion and solving customers' biggest problems across the region.

With over two decades of Australian and international consulting and channel experience, Josh has delivered outcomes for customers and partners across various industries. His expertise spans commercial management, digital transformation, information strategy and governance, solution architecture, and organizational change management.

Josh holds an MBA with Honours from the University of Western Australia (WAM 82.4) and was awarded the Tracey Horton Scholar award. As a Graduate and Member of the Australian Institute of Company Directors (GAICD), he brings strong business acumen and governance expertise to DDD Perth, ensuring the organization remains financially sustainable while delivering exceptional value to the Perth software community.

## Past committees

DDD Perth was started in 2015 by Rob Moore and Matt Davies, who ran it together until a wider group joined in 2017. In
late 2017 the conference was incorporated as DDD WA Inc., and since then each year's committee has been elected at the
association's annual general meeting. These are the committees elected at each AGM, plus directors the committee appointed
later in the year to fill vacant roles.
